# Unresolved club pairs

Pairs that look like one club written two ways, where the evidence does not
settle it. They are **pinned as distinct** in `scripts/team_normalizer.py`
(`KNOWN_DISTINCT`), so neither the spelling rule nor the containment pass can
fold them together. Do not merge one of these without resolving the question
below first, and if you do resolve it, say so here.

## Libertas Forlì / Fulgor Libertas Forlì

| | Libertas Forlì | Fulgor Libertas Forlì |
|---|---|---|
| Place | Forlì, Emilia-Romagna, Italy | Forlì, Emilia-Romagna, Italy |

**The case for one club:** the names, and the city. Italian clubs routinely
carry a sponsor or a merger partner in front of the historical name, and
"Fulgor Libertas Forlì" reads exactly like Libertas Forlì after a merger with
Fulgor. The containment pass would fold them on that basis alone.

**The case for two:** the stints overlap for thirteen years. A club that
became another club does not field two rosters for over a decade. Either these
are two clubs in the same city, or our year data on one of them is wrong.

**What would settle it:** the founding and dissolution years of both clubs,
and whether the Italian league tables for the overlapping seasons list one
entry or two. Until someone checks that, they stay two records.

Recorded 2026-09-22, after the place audit.
