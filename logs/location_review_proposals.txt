Source: 1224 teams with a location-less stint. Two HIGH-confidence signals:
(a) this exact team-name string already has a located stint elsewhere in the
data, reused directly; (b) the team name contains a city name that resolves
to exactly one country everywhere else it's used in this dataset, is not
slash-joined, and isn't preceded by an article/preposition that could mean a
dropped compound-name word (the "Los Lagos" -> "Lagos" trap).

A same-city-name match being statistically unambiguous WITHIN this dataset is
not the same as being factually correct -- a manual real-world-knowledge pass
over the full candidate set caught and excluded several matches that looked
safe by the numbers but were wrong: "Club de Regatas Lima" is a Peruvian
club, not Lima, Ohio; "Phoenix Fuel Masters" is a Philippine PBA team, not
Phoenix, AZ; "Newcastle Eagles" is English, not Australian; "Universidad de
Los Lagos" is Chilean, not Lagos, Nigeria; "Rosalía de Castro" is a Galician
poet's name (the club is Spanish), not Castro, Chile. These, plus a short
list of other known cross-country homonym cities (Newark, Hamilton, Beverly
Hills, Worcester, etc.), are excluded from HIGH regardless of what the
in-dataset statistics alone would suggest -- see the MEDIUM section below.

Three team-name strings contain literal HTML/wikitext comment syntax
("<!--"/"-->") -- these are upstream parsing artifacts, not real team names,
and are called out separately rather than backfilled under a fake name.
